About The ONE Gym

Built for people who take training seriously.

The ONE Gym is Yerevan's premium training destination, built around one idea: excellence without compromise. Every machine, every plate and every corner of our space is chosen for performance and comfort. Our coaching staff trains with genuine care, our environment stays immaculate, and every member gets a space engineered for their goals.

  • Latest professional equipment, maintained to a five-star standard
  • A clean, meticulously kept environment, always
  • A friendly, welcoming atmosphere for every level
  • Professional coaching staff invested in your progress
